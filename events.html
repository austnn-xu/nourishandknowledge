The weekly rhythm

What is coming up

The same three gatherings run all year. Turn up to any of them without telling us first.

01

Community Supper

Friday evenings

A free, home-cooked meal and a long table. No RSVP, no cost, and leftovers go home with whoever wants them.

02

Story & Snack Hour

Saturday mornings

Read-alouds, crafts, and a proper snack for little ones and the grown-ups who brought them.

03

Cook & Learn

Once a month

A hands-on workshop where we cook one meal together, eat it, and send everyone home with the recipe and the pantry list.
