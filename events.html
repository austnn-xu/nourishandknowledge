What to expect

First time? Here’s what we can tell you so far.

Do I need to sign up?
Not for Community Supper, which needs no RSVP at all. For Cook & Learn workshops, send us a note and we’ll let you know about the next one.
Is there a cost?
No. Our meals and workshops are free, and there are no forms to fill in and no questions asked.
Can I bring my kids?
Please do. Our tables are built for families, and Story & Snack Hour exists so younger neighbors have something of their own.
I’d rather help than eat. Can I volunteer?
Yes. Send us a note and we’ll find a way for you to pitch in.
