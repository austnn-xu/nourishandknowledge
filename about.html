About Us

The people, the story, and the heart behind Nourish & Knowledge.

Nourish & Knowledge started around a single kitchen table, with a handful of neighbors who noticed that folks nearby were going hungry — for food, and for a place to belong. So we started cooking, and we started sharing what we knew. People kept showing up, and we never stopped.

Who we are

We're a small but mighty crew of volunteers, cooks, teachers, and friends who simply like taking care of people. There's no fancy secret to what we do — just good food, open hearts, and the belief that everyone deserves both.
