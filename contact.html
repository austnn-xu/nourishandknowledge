Quick answers

I need food support. What should I say?
As little or as much as you’re comfortable with. There are no forms and no questions asked, and you can simply come to a Friday supper. If it’s easier to ask privately, choose “I need support” above.
Can I volunteer if I only have an hour?
An hour is genuinely useful. Setting up, serving, reading a story, or washing up at the end all matter, and none of it requires a standing commitment.
Prefer to just turn up?
That works too. Community Supper runs every Friday evening, with no note needed and no RSVP.
