What we do

Three things, done well

Fed and curious people build stronger neighborhoods. That belief shapes every program we run.

01

Nourish

Hot meals and fresh groceries for families who need them. No forms, no eligibility test, no questions asked.

02

Learn

Free workshops, tutoring, and story hours for learners of every age, run by neighbors who know the material.

03

Belong

Open tables where neighbors meet, cook together, and keep coming back long after the plates are cleared.

Our belief

Nobody should have to choose between groceries and the rent.
Catherine Liu, Founder
