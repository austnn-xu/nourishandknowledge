Why partner with us

Small organization, short distance to impact

We’re volunteer-run and local, which means there’s very little between what you give and the family who receives it. No layers, no long approval chains, just neighbors getting something done.

Good things to know

How big does a partner need to be?
Any size at all. A single bakery donating end-of-day bread makes a real difference, and so does a district-wide school program. We shape the partnership around what you can comfortably give.
What kinds of contributions are most useful?
Food and ingredients, a space to gather, transport, volunteer hours from your team, or funding for a specific program. Skills count too, including tutoring, translation, bookkeeping, and design.
How do we get started?
Send us a note with a little about your organization and what you have in mind, and we’ll take it from there.

Let’s start a conversation

We love meeting kindred spirits. Tell us a little about you, and we’ll find the perfect way to team up.
